# 2. Standardize on Gardner Staging and ESHRE Morphokinetic Intervals

Date: 2026-02-10

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Reproductive medicine requires standardized, reproducible grading protocols across European and global clinics to prevent subjective inter-observer variability between embryologists.

## Decision
We enforce the standard **Gardner & Schoolcraft Staging Criteria** (Expansion 1-6, ICM A-C, TE A-C) combined with ESHRE Vienna Consensus time-lapse morphokinetic intervals ($cc_2 = t_3 - t_2$, $s_2 = t_4 - t_3$).

## Consequences
- Directly compatible with clinical laboratory information systems (LIMS).
- Fulfills EU AI Act clinical benchmarking and validation standards.
